The show

All About central virginia

I Love Virginia takes the commonwealth's iconic places, weird local legends, unforgettable events, and deep history — and cuts them into quick-hit, first-person interviews. Fast, funny, and unmistakably local. Think of it as the state's greatest hits, told by the people who actually live them.

A pop culture clip show reminicent of early MTV / VH1.
